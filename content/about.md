I'm Neelima — a hiker, adventurer, and explorer who believes that the world's most extraordinary places are best experienced on foot. Over the past decade I've trekked to Everest Base Camp, summited Kilimanjaro, crossed Patagonia, and explored fjords, canyons, and glaciers across four continents.

This site is a living record of those journeys — the landscapes, the people, the exhaustion, and the extraordinary privilege of standing somewhere few people ever stand. Each expedition has changed the way I see the world and my place in it.

I also fundraise for charitable causes through these adventures. The Everest Base Camp expedition was completed to raise $30,000 — with the incredible support of sponsors and friends who believed in the mission.

The mountains have a way of stripping everything back to what matters. Out there, on a high ridge with the world spread below you, the noise of ordinary life falls completely away. That's why I keep going back.
