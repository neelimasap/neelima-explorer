Nestled deep in the Canadian Rockies, Banff National Park is one of the most breathtaking places on earth. With turquoise glacier-fed lakes, towering limestone peaks, and vast alpine wilderness, Banff offers an unmatched blend of natural beauty and outdoor adventure.

Whether you're hiking to the shores of Lake Louise, paddling on Moraine Lake, or spotting wildlife along the Bow Valley Parkway, every corner of this UNESCO World Heritage Site leaves you speechless.

The colour of the lakes — that impossible turquoise from glacial rock flour suspended in the water — is something no photograph fully captures. This was one of the most memorable trips of my life — a place I will return to again and again.
